## Overview

The simwalk2 skill is designed for stochastic statistical analysis of qualitative traits. It is particularly useful for researchers in population genetics and quantitative genetics who need to perform complex simulations and statistical modeling to understand the inheritance and evolution of traits within populations.

## Usage Instructions

simwalk2 is a command-line tool. Its usage typically involves specifying input parameters that define the simulation scenario, population structure, and traits of interest.

While detailed parameter documentation is not provided in the source material, common patterns for such tools suggest the following:

*   **Trait Definition**: Specify how qualitative traits are defined and inherited.
